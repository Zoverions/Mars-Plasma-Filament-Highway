# Fractal Transport System: Phased Roadmap (2026–2040)

This document outlines the strategic plan for developing and deploying the Mars Plasma-Filament Highway, starting from terrestrial prototypes to full interplanetary infrastructure.

## Phase 1: Foundation & Earth Prototypes (2026–2028)

**Objective**: Validate core physics and build the Toronto vacuum-tube feeder.

*   **2026 Q2**: Publication of core theory (this repository) and initial peer review.
*   **2026 Q4**: Laboratory demonstration of meter-scale plasma waveguides with subluminal group velocity control.
*   **2027**: Construction of the **Toronto Vacuum Feeder Test Track** (5 km segment).
    *   *Location*: Parallel to GO Transit corridor.
    *   *Goal*: Demonstrate 300 m/s maglev transport of dummy payloads.
*   **2028**: Completion of full 50km feeder prototype.
    *   Validation of vacuum seal integrity and exit-velocity consistency.

## Phase 2: Orbital Infrastructure & Highway Activation (2029–2032)

**Objective**: Establish LEO nodes and initiate the first plasma filament.

*   **2029**: Launch of **Pathfinder Beacons** to LEO and Mars Orbit.
    *   These satellites serve as the endpoints for the laser-ionized channel.
*   **2030**: **First Light**. Activation of the high-power laser system to ionize the Earth-Mars corridor.
    *   Creation of the initial "seed" filament.
    *   Measurement of particle drag reduction within the channel.
*   **2031**: Deployment of magnetic confinement relay stations at Lagrange points.
*   **2032**: Full highway stabilization.
    *   Channel diameter expanded to accommodate standard AI data center modules.

## Phase 3: First Transport & Expansion (2033–2035)

**Objective**: Transport operational AI hardware to Mars.

*   **2033**: **Mission Alpha**. Launch of the first AI Data Center module from Toronto.
    *   Trajectory: Earth -> Plasma Highway -> Mars Orbit.
    *   Transit time: ~134 days.
*   **2034**: Arrival and braking at Mars.
    *   Module enters Mars orbit and begins data relay operations.
*   **2035**: Regular cargo schedule established.
    *   Bi-annual launches of compute infrastructure.

## Phase 4: Scaling & Human Transit (2036–2040)

**Objective**: Scale the system for massive cargo and potentially biological payloads.

*   **2036**: Parallel highways established for redundancy.
*   **2038**: Introduction of larger "Habitat Modules" for biological transport trials (cellular level).
*   **2040**: The **Fractal Network** connects Earth, Mars, and the Belt.
    *   A continuous web of low-resistance corridors facilitating a multi-planetary economy.
